# Third-party data notices

## Car manufacturer logos

The offline manufacturer-logo catalog is synchronized from
[`filippofilip95/car-logos-dataset`](https://github.com/filippofilip95/car-logos-dataset).
The repository describes the dataset tooling as MIT licensed. Vehicle logos and
brand names remain trademarks and property of their respective owners and are
displayed only to identify vehicle makes inside the parts catalog.

## Vehicle makes and models

Vehicle model names are synchronized from the U.S. Department of
Transportation NHTSA vPIC API: <https://vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov/api/>.

The generated catalog is a starter/reference catalog, not a fitment authority.
Users can add or correct local models, generations, engines and year ranges in
the application. Part fitment should always be verified against the vehicle VIN,
OEM part number or the supplier/manufacturer catalog before sale.
